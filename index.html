About Me

I am a PhD student at the University of Edinburgh on the UKRI CDT in Natural Language Processing programme. My research interests include multilinguality in large language models, text generation, decoding strategies, hallucinations, machine translation, and data augmentation and filtering.

After completing a Master's degree in Physics at the University of Liverpool, I worked as a software developer for three years. I then completed a Master's degree in Data Science at the University of Edinburgh. In 2021, I commenced my PhD at the University of Edinburgh, supervised by Alexandra Birch and Barry Haddow.

Research Interests

  • Large Language Models: Multilingual Representation Learning, Synthetic Data Generation for Multilingual Tasks, and Language Coverage in Multilingual LLMs
  • Machine Learning: Text Generation, Decoding Methods, Hallucinations, and Reinforcement Learning
  • Machine Translation: Low-resource Translation, Transfer Learning, and Data Augmentation
